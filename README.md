# Offence IQ — South Australian Legislation Trainer

**Know the law. Recall the law.**

Offence IQ is an independent, mobile-friendly study tool for South Australian legislation. This build replaces the earlier scenario-assessment concept with objective legislative learning, statutory recall, flashcards, knowledge tests and progress tracking.

## Build 1.0 features
- Responsive mobile-first home screen and navigation
- Legislation library with Criminal Law Consolidation Act 1935 (SA), s 20 (Assault), and Summary Offences Act 1953 (SA), s 7 (Disorderly or offensive conduct or language)
- Statutory wording, section references, source links and verification note
- Search within each module, highlight matches and jump to matching subsections
- Five flashcards per module with separate mastery tracking
- Randomised objective knowledge tests with explanations
- Local progress and accuracy tracking
- PWA manifest and service worker for installability/offline app shell support

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Open **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/(root)**, then save.
4. Open the published Pages URL. On Android Chrome, use **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**, if offered).

## Legal source and accuracy policy
- AustLII is the preferred readable research source when locating and studying legislation.
- Cross-check every legal proposition and statutory extract against the current authorised version on the [South Australian Legislation website](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/).
- Preserve the Act title, section/subsection, exact statutory language where quoted, current status/version, verification date and applicable penalty.
- Clearly distinguish verbatim legislation from simplified study explanations.
- Flag amendments, commencement questions, discrepancies and matters requiring qualified legal or agency review.
- Do not treat this app as SAPOL policy, operational direction or legal advice.

## Privacy
Quiz statistics and flashcard mastery are stored in browser local storage on this device. There is no account system, cloud sync or analytics in this build.
